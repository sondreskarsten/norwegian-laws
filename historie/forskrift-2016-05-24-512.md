# Endringshistorikk: forskrift/2016-05-24-512

### 2020-10-07 — forskrift/2020-10-07-1985

*I forskrift 24. mai 2016 nr. 512 Operational Directive requiring the use of pressurized aircraft for air operators conducting scheduled passenger flights over mountainous areas in Norway gjøres følgende endring:*

> I forskrift 24. mai 2016 nr. 512 Operational Directive requiring the use of pressurized aircraft for air operators conducting scheduled passenger flights over mountainous areas in Norway gjøres følgende endring:
>
> Punkt 1.1 skal lyde:
>
> This Operational Directive contains mandatory action that is required to establish an acceptable level of safety. The Directive is issued in accordance with the Aviation Act (luftfartsloven) § 9-1.
>
> Forskriften trer i kraft straks.
